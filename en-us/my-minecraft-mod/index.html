Peach Crisp

Add Chinese-style treats to your Minecraft world

A lightweight Fabric mod that adds Peach Crisp and Golden Peach Crisp to Minecraft. Peach Crisp grants Regeneration when eaten, while Golden Peach Crisp restores more hunger and provides powerful buffs like Haste and Resistance — plus it attracts Piglins!
