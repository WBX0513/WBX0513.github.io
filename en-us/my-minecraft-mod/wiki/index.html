📖 Overview

Peach Crisp is a lightweight Fabric mod that adds two food items to Minecraft: Peach Crisp and Golden Peach Crisp. The mod enriches vanilla survival gameplay with new food options and combat-supporting effects.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.1.2, uses the Fabric loader, requires installation on both client and server, and is licensed under MIT.

🍪 Items

Peach Crisp

A basic food item. Restores 2 hunger points (1 bar) and 0.2 saturation when eaten, and grants Regeneration II for 3 seconds.

Golden Peach Crisp

An upgraded version of Peach Crisp. Restores 8 hunger points (4 bars) and 1.2 saturation when eaten, and grants multiple powerful buffs. Golden Peach Crisp is included in the #minecraft:piglin_loved tag, so Piglins are attracted to it.

🐷 Piglin Interaction

Golden Peach Crisp is listed under the #minecraft:piglin_loved item tag, meaning Piglins will notice Golden Peach Crisps on the ground and attempt to pick them up.

Note: When a Piglin picks up a Golden Peach Crisp, it will examine the item (performing the bartering animation), but will not drop any bartering loot — it simply keeps the Golden Peach Crisp. If you kill that Piglin, the Golden Peach Crisp is guaranteed to drop.
